Hi, I'm Alex.

Mechatronics Engineer, R&D at Unifiller.

I design and develop food-production equipment in R&D at Unifiller, where a single machine pulls in mechanical design, electronics, firmware and controls. That mix is what I enjoy most. I like working across disciplines, and I keep pushing deeper into mechanical design.

Robots are the thread through all of it. I studied Mechatronic Systems Engineering at Simon Fraser University, and these projects span embedded firmware, control systems and product development.

Mechanical design has my attention right now: CAD in SolidWorks, mechanisms, and sizing parts for real loads. I'm also fascinated by robots and build them on my own time. I studied Mechatronic Systems Engineering at Simon Fraser University.

Projects

Dynamic Motion Interface for Game Control

Turned an STM32 board into a tilt controller for a Python 2D shooter. Wrote the SPI driver for the onboard accelerometer and used timers and interrupts to stream motion data to the game over USB.

  • STM32F407VG
  • C
  • SPI
  • GPIO

Design and Control of a Two-wheel Self-Balancing Robot

Modeled the dynamics of a two-wheel self-balancing robot and designed its feedback controller in MATLAB and Simulink. Used state-space analysis of controllability and observability to reach stable balancing, then built a 3D Simscape simulation of the robot.

  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • Simscape
  • Feedback Control
  • State-Space Modeling

Foot Wearable for Dystonia Pain Management (Capstone)

Led controls for Lief, a wearable that eases pain from foot dystonia. Wrote firmware driving two coin vibration motors in a foot wrap with PWM intensity control, built the circuit on protoboard, and made a desktop GUI so users can track their sessions.

  • C
  • Arduino
  • Soldering
  • Python
  • PySide
