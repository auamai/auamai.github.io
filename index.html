Projects

Poundo Robot In progress

Poundo yam is a West African staple and my favourite food. It's also tedious to make: you stir yam flour into hot water and keep turning it hard with a wooden stick until it's smooth and stretchy. I'm building a robot arm that learns that turning motion from videos of people making it. Right now I'm simulating an SO-101 arm in MuJoCo with Jacobian IK, PD control and contact-force sensing.

  • MuJoCo
  • LeRobot
  • SO-101
  • Jacobian IK
  • Contact sensing
