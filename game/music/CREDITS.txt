Edit made for the game: a 90.000 s loop (36 bars at 96 BPM) cut from
82.52 s to 172.52 s of the original, with a 20 ms equal-power crossfade
baked into the loop end, 0.25 s of wrap-around padding on each side
(the player loops 0.25 s .. 90.25 s), -4.9 dB static gain to about
-16 LUFS integrated, AAC-LC 128 kbps stereo 44.1 kHz.

The synthesized "Line Shift" cue in audio.js is original to the game and
is used as a fallback when this file cannot be loaded.
